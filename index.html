Human-Like Rewriting

Rewrite the following text to match a specific human and natural tone. Use clear language. Use a conversational and slightly informal tone. Avoid predictable rhythmic patterns. Avoid robotic structure. Insert specific personal anecdotes or hypothetical examples to illustrate points. Use common idioms correctly. Avoid repetitive transition words. If a sentence sounds too perfect or formal, break it. Write as if you are explaining this to a friend over coffee. Readability should match a college student. Prioritize clarity over complex vocabulary. Remove any list that looks too structured. Integrate those points into narrative paragraphs instead. Ensure the text includes occasional subjective opinions or mild biases typical of a human writer.

Research & Citations

Finding Reliable References

I am working on [insert your topic]. Please help me find reliable references that I can use for research.

Requirements:
• Sources must be credible and authoritative (e.g., peer-reviewed journals, academic books, government reports, or reputable organizations).
• Provide full bibliographic details (author, title, publication, year, and link if available).
• Summarize each reference in 2–3 sentences, highlighting its main argument, findings, or relevance to my topic.
• Include a mix of recent publications (last 5–10 years) and foundational works if they exist.
• Organize references by themes or subtopics (e.g., causes, effects, solutions).
• Format output in a clear, structured way (e.g., numbered list or table).
